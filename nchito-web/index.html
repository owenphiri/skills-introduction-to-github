The rails exist. The work doesn't reach people.

Mobile money already reaches most Zambian adults, and plenty of people are looking for extra income. What's missing is somewhere trustworthy to find the work — and a way to prove you did it well.

🔍

Work is found by word of mouth

Which means it reaches whoever you already know. A good cleaner two streets away and a family who needs one may never hear of each other.

🤝

Nobody wants to pay first

The poster fears paying for work that never happens. The worker fears working for money that never comes. Both hesitate, and the job doesn't happen.

📄

Years of good work prove nothing

A cleaner with eight years of excellent service has nothing to show a new employer, a bank or a landlord. The record simply doesn't exist.

💸

Cash pays today

That's the honest reason people choose cash jobs. Any platform that makes you wait until the end of the week is competing with something better.

What makes Nchito different

Not features for their own sake — each of these answers one of the problems above.

📄

A work record you own

Every job paid through Nchito is added to a signed, tamper-evident history — what you did, for whom, rated how, on time or not. Export it as a CV, or share a link an employer can check. It can't be self-reported, and nobody can quietly edit it. Not even us.

⚡

Get paid before the job ends

Once you've started the work, take up to half your payout immediately for a flat fee. It isn't a loan — the money is already sitting in escrow, and this just releases part of it early. It's how we compete with cash.

📞

Works without a smartphone

Dial a shortcode from any handset — no app, no data bundle — to browse gigs, apply, check your balance and cash out. Or just message Nchito on WhatsApp, where people already coordinate work.

📍

Find agents that actually have cash

A balance you can't withdraw isn't money. Nchito maps which mobile-money agents have float right now, reported by other workers — and says plainly when nobody has checked recently, rather than sending you on a wasted trip.

📉

Our fee falls the longer you stay

10% at first, then 7%, then 5% as a poster and worker keep working together. Most platforms take the same cut forever, which quietly pushes people to settle in cash. We'd rather earn less and keep the work on the platform.

📷

Proof, not arguments

Before-and-after photos, stamped with the time and place they were taken, are attached to the job. Payment can't be released without them — and they settle most disputes before they start.

How the money moves

Nobody has to trust anybody. The platform holds the money in between.

The poster funds escrow

The full amount is held by Nchito before work begins, so the worker knows the money is real.

The worker starts, and proves it

A geotagged "before" photo goes on the job. From here, early payment becomes available.

The work is finished and photographed

The "after" photo completes the record. Without both, payment can't be released at all.

The poster confirms, and money moves

The worker is paid minus our fee, the job joins their work record, and any early payment is settled automatically.

Where the project is

Nchito is in development, not yet launched. The apps, the database, the USSD and WhatsApp layers and this demo are all built; what's outstanding is connecting real mobile-money disbursement through a licensed provider, identity verification, and the regulatory groundwork that has to come before anyone's actual money moves.

The demo uses sample gigs and sample people. No real payments are processed, and no figure shown in it is a real transaction.
