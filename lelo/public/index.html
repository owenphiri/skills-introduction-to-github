What we will not do

  • We do not message you without permission. Nothing is sent until you opt in, and STOP ends it the same day.
  • We do not sell your number. Your number and district stay with us.
  • We do not give medical advice. Health messages are general public-health information from the Ministry of Health. For anything about your own body, see a clinic.
  • We never ask for your PIN. Nobody from Lelo will ever ask for it. Anyone who does is a thief.
