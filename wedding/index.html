A Love Written in the Stars

Our Story

❦

Some love stories whisper; ours arrived boldly. Owen and Beauty first met at a church fellowship in Kasama — he was the confident voice leading the discussion, she was the radiant smile that made him forget his next point. One conversation after the service turned into hours, and hours turned into a friendship neither of us wanted to end.

Through seasons of laughter, prayer, long phone calls and shared dreams, we discovered that we are better, braver and kinder together. Owen brought the boldness; Beauty brought the beauty — and God wove the two into one story.

On a quiet evening overlooking the Chishimba Falls, Owen knelt down and asked the question he had carried in his heart for years. Beauty said yes before he could finish. Now we joyfully invite you, our family and friends, to witness the day we become one — The Bold & The Beautiful.
